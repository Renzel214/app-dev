## The Walking Dead Overview
**The series features a large ensemble cast as survivors of a zombie apocalypse trying to stay alive under near-constant threat of attacks from zombies known as "walkers". With the collapse of modern civilization, these survivors must confront other human survivors who have formed groups and communities with their own sets of laws and morals, sometimes leading to open conflict between them.**

### Why I Love this Series
Because it uses a zombie apocalypse not just as a monster thriller, but as a deep psychological experiment on human nature. Also, it shows how can a group of strangers become family at the end.

## Zombieland Overview
**is a post-apocalyptic horror-comedy film that follows a quirky, risk-averse college student named Columbus as he navigates a world overrun by zombies alongside three other survivors. Guided by a strict set of survival rules, Columbus reluctantly teams up with Tallahassee, a gun-toting, Twinkie-obsessed badass, and a pair of cunning con-artist sisters, Wichita and Little Rock. Together, this makeshift family embarks on a chaotic cross-country road trip toward an alleged safe haven in Los Angeles, encountering hordes of undead, an iconic celebrity cameo, and a series of explosive, amusement-park showdowns along the way. The film blends gory, fast-paced action with sharp wit and heartwarming themes about finding family in the bleakest of times.**
